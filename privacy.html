Full Shot Master — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Full Shot Master is a Chrome extension for capturing complete, scrollable webpages and editing, annotating, exporting, copying, or optionally sharing the captured screenshot.

1. Information the Extension Handles

  • Website content: webpage content is captured when you explicitly start a full-page screenshot.
  • Webpage information: the captured screenshot may be associated with the webpage title and URL.
  • Telegram bot credentials: if you choose to use Telegram sharing, you may provide your own Telegram bot token and chat ID.
  • Screenshot data: the captured screenshot may be temporarily stored locally so you can edit, export, copy, or share it.

2. How Data Is Used

The data handled by Full Shot Master is used only to provide its core screenshot functionality and related features, including full-page capture, editing, annotation, PNG/PDF export, clipboard copying, and optional Telegram sharing.

3. Local Processing and Storage

Screenshot processing and editing are performed locally in the extension. Screenshot data and extension settings are stored locally in your browser. Telegram bot credentials, when configured, are stored locally using Chrome extension storage.

4. Telegram Sharing

Telegram sharing is optional and is not automatic. If you configure your own Telegram bot and explicitly click the Telegram sharing button, the selected screenshot is sent directly to the Telegram Bot API at https://api.telegram.org/ using the credentials you provided.

Full Shot Master does not send screenshots to Telegram unless you choose the Telegram sharing feature.

5. Data Sharing and Sale

Full Shot Master does not sell user data. The extension does not use analytics or advertising scripts. User data is not transferred for purposes unrelated to the extension's single purpose.

6. Data Retention and Removal

Locally stored extension data remains in the browser until it is cleared by the extension, removed through the extension's available settings, or removed by clearing the extension's browser storage. Telegram bot settings can be cleared from the Telegram Bot Settings section.

7. Security

Full Shot Master is designed to process screenshot data locally whenever possible. No security measure can guarantee absolute security, so users should avoid capturing sensitive information unless they understand the risks of storing or sharing that information.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the extension's functionality or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions about Full Shot Master, please contact the extension developer through the contact information provided on the extension's Chrome Web Store listing.
